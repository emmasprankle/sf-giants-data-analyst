## Logan Webb — SP (Ace)

**Role:** Franchise starter, innings anchor  
**Throws:** Right  
**2024 line:** 204.2 IP · ERA 3.47 · FIP 2.95 · xFIP 3.28 · xERA 4.37 · 4.4 WAR  
**Ratios:** 7.56 K/9 · 2.20 BB/9 · 0.48 HR/9 · 56.8% GB% · 71.7% LOB%  
**Plate discipline:** SwStr% 8.5% · CSW% 28.4%  
**Arsenal:** Sinker 38% · Changeup 24% · Sweeper 21% · Four-seam 10% · Cutter 8%  
**Strength:** Elite ground ball rate and command. FIP (2.95) is legitimately elite — the sinker/changeup combination generates weak contact and limits barrels.  
**Limitation:** xERA (4.37) reads higher than ERA because Statcast sees more hard contact than outcomes show — Oracle Park's park effect and ground ball profile partially mask exit velocity on balls that find gloves. Low SwStr% (8.5%) means he's not a swing-and-miss pitcher; command and contact quality are the profile.

## Blake Snell — SP

**Role:** Mid-season acquisition, strikeout specialist  
**Throws:** Left  
**2024 line:** 104.0 IP · ERA 3.12 · FIP 2.43 · xFIP 3.01 · xERA 2.55 · 3.1 WAR  
**Ratios:** 12.55 K/9 · high BB/9  
**Plate discipline:** SwStr% 16.3% · CSW% 32.8%  
**Arsenal:** Four-seam fastball + breaking ball dominant  
**Strength:** The Giants' only elite swing-and-miss starter. SwStr% (16.3%) and CSW% (32.8%) are well above staff average. FIP (2.43) reflects genuine strikeout dominance.  
**Limitation:** Walk rate is the primary concern — high BB/9 runs counts up and limits innings efficiency. Small sample (104.0 IP) limits reliability of the metrics.

## Jordan Hicks — SP

## Kyle Harrison — SP

**Role:** Homegrown starting pitcher  
**Throws:** Left  
**2024 line:** 124.1 IP · ERA 4.56 · FIP 4.33 · xFIP 4.23 · 0.8 WAR  
**Ratios:** 12.3% HR/FB  
**Plate discipline:** High K% potential (left-handed with swing-and-miss stuff)  
**Strength:** Plus stuff from the left side — the Giants' best homegrown pitching prospect. High strikeout upside when command is on.  
**Limitation:** HR/FB rate (12.3%) is the primary weakness. Fly balls become home runs at an above-average clip. Oracle Park partially masks this at home, but road starts expose real HR vulnerability. FIP and ERA convergence indicates the struggles are genuine, not bad luck.

## Hayden Birdsong — SP

**Role:** Rotation depth, call-up  
**Throws:** Right  
**2024 line:** 72.0 IP · ERA 4.75 · FIP 4.67 · 0.1 WAR  
**Ratios:** 15.1% HR/FB · 11.0 K/9

## Ryan Walker — RP

**Role:** High-leverage reliever  
**Throws:** Right  
**2024 line:** 80.0 IP · ERA 1.91 · FIP 2.52 · xFIP 2.80 · xERA 2.61 · 1.7 WAR  
**Ratios:** 46.9% GB% · 82.9% LOB%  
**Plate discipline:** SwStr% 12.4% · CSW% 33.6%  
**Strength:** Deceptive delivery generates significant swing-and-miss and weak contact. FIP (2.52) and xERA (2.61) confirm the skill is real — this is not purely a LOB% mirage.  
**Limitation:** LOB% (82.9%) is unsustainable — ERA will likely regress toward FIP (2.52–2.80) range in 2025, not because Walker gets worse but because strand rate normalizes. Analysts should project him near his FIP, not his ERA.

## Camilo Doval — CL

**Role:** Closer  
**Throws:** Right  
**2024 line:** 59.0 IP · 23 SV · ERA 4.88 · FIP 3.71 · xERA 3.32 · 0.1 WAR  
**Ratios:** 60.0% GB% · 99.5 mph vFA  
**Strength:** Elite fastball velocity (99.5 mph) and the highest ground ball rate on the staff (60.0% GB%). Underlying contact quality (xERA 3.32) is solid — the pitch mix and velocity profile are legitimately valuable.  
**Limitation:** ERA (4.88) vs. xERA (3.32) gap of 1.56 runs is the largest on the staff — almost entirely a strand rate story. Doval allowed runners to score at an unusually high rate given the contact quality. WAR (0.1) is low for a closer, but the underlying metrics suggest he was better than ERA implies. Mean reversion in LOB% is the most likely path back to closer-caliber ERA.

## Tyler Rogers — RP

**Role:** Specialist reliever (submarine delivery)  
**Throws:** Right  
**2024 line:** 70.1 IP · ERA 2.82 · FIP 3.56 · 0.6 WAR  
**Ratios:** 54.5% GB% · 0.77 BB/9  
**Strength:** Submarine delivery creates extreme deception against right-handed hitters. Walk rate (0.77 BB/9) is exceptional — one of the best command profiles on the staff. Ground ball rate (54.5%) reflects the extreme angle of the delivery. ERA (2.82) below FIP (3.56) suggests some favorable strand rate, but the command and deception are genuinely elite.  
**Limitation:** Platoon split is significant — submarine delivery is far less effective against left-handed hitters. Usage should be matchup-driven rather than role-based.
