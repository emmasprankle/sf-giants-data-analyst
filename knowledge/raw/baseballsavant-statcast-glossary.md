## Statcast Search CSV Documentation

This is the documentation for the Statcast Search CSV data downloads.

### [pitch\_type](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#pitch_type)

The type of pitch derived from Statcast.

### [game\_date](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#game_date)

Date of the Game.

### [release\_speed](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#release_speed)

Pitch velocity in MPH.
Values from 2008-16 are via PitchFX, and adjusted to roughly out-of-hand release point.
All velocities from 2017 and beyond are Statcast, which are reported out-of-hand.
All velocities are thus on the same scale.


### [release\_pos\_x](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#release_pos_x)

Horizontal Release Position of the ball measured in feet from the catcher's perspective.

### [release\_pos\_z](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#release_pos_z)

Vertical Release Position of the ball measured in feet from the catcher's perspective.

### [player\_name](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#player_name)

Player's name tied to the event of the search.

### [batter](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#batter)

MLB Player Id tied to the play event.

### [pitcher](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#pitcher)

MLB Player Id tied to the play event.

### [events](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#events)

Event of the resulting Plate Appearance.

### [description](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#description)

Description of the resulting pitch.

### [spin\_dir](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#spin_dir)

\\* Deprecated field from the old tracking system.

### [spin\_rate\_deprecated](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#spin_rate_deprecated)

\\* Deprecated field from the old tracking system. Replaced by [release\_spin](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs#release_spin)

### [break\_angle\_deprecated](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#break_angle_deprecated)

\\* Deprecated field from the old tracking system.

### [break\_length\_deprecated](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#break_length_deprecated)

\\* Deprecated field from the old tracking system.

### [zone](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#zone)

Zone location of the ball when it crosses the plate from the catcher's perspective.

![](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/sections/statcast-search-new/images/zones.png)

### [des](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#des)

Plate appearance description from game day.

### [game\_type](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#game_type)

Type of Game. E = Exhibition, S = Spring Training, R = Regular Season, F = Wild Card, D = Divisional Series, L = League Championship Series, W = World Series

### [stand](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#stand)

Side of the plate batter is standing.

### [p\_throws](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#p_throws)

Hand pitcher throws with.

### [home\_team](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#home_team)

Abbreviation of home team.

### [away\_team](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#away_team)

Abbreviation of away team.

### [type](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#type)

Short hand of pitch result. B = ball, S = strike, X = in play.

### [hit\_location](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#hit_location)

Position of first fielder to touch the ball.

### [bb\_type](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#bb_type)

Batted ball type, ground\_ball, line\_drive, fly\_ball, popup.

### [balls](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#balls)

Pre-pitch number of balls in count.

### [strikes](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#strikes)

Pre-pitch number of strikes in count.

### [game\_year](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#game_year)

Year game took place.

### [pfx\_x](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#pfx_x)

Horizontal movement in feet from the catcher's perspective.

### [pfx\_z](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#pfx_z)

Vertical movement in feet from the catcher's perpsective.

### [plate\_x](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#plate_x)

Horizontal position of the ball when it crosses home plate from the catcher's perspective.
Through 2025, this was front-of-plate.
From 2026 on, this is middle-of-plate to align with the ABS system.


### [plate\_z](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#plate_z)

Vertical position of the ball when it crosses home plate from the catcher's perspective.
Through 2025, this was front-of-plate.
From 2026 on, this is middle-of-plate to align with the ABS system.


### [on\_3b](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#on_3b)

Pre-pitch MLB Player Id of Runner on 3B.

### [on\_2b](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#on_2b)

Pre-pitch MLB Player Id of Runner on 2B.

### [on\_1b](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#on_1b)

### [hc\_y](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#hc_y)

The velocity of the pitch, in feet per second, in x-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

### [vy0](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#vy0)

The velocity of the pitch, in feet per second, in y-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

### [vy0](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#vy0)

The velocity of the pitch, in feet per second, in z-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

The acceleration of the pitch, in feet per second per second, in x-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

### [ay](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#ay)

The acceleration of the pitch, in feet per second per second, in y-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

### [az](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#az)

The acceleration of the pitch, in feet per second per second, in z-dimension, determined at y=50 feet.

### [sz\_top](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#sz_top)

Top of the batter's strike zone set by the operator when the ball is halfway to the plate.
Through 2025, top of the batter's strike zone set by the operator when the ball is halfway to the plate.
From 2026 on, this is the top of the batter's ABS-defined strike zone.


### [sz\_bot](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#sz_bot)

Bottom of the batter's strike zone set by the operator when the ball is halfway to the plate.
Through 2025, bottom of the batter's strike zone set by the operator when the ball is halfway to the plate.
From 2026 on, this is the bottom of the batter's ABS-defined strike zone.


### [hit\_distance](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#hit_distance)

Projected hit distance of the batted ball.

### [launch\_speed](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#launch_speed)

Exit velocity of the batted ball as tracked by Statcast. For the limited subset of batted balls not tracked directly, estimates are included based on the process described [here](http://tangotiger.com/index.php/site/article/statcast-lab-no-nulls-in-batted-balls-launch-parameters).

### [launch\_angle](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#launch_angle)

Launch angle of the batted ball as tracked by Statcast. For the limited subset of batted balls not tracked directly, estimates are included based on the process described [here](http://tangotiger.com/index.php/site/article/statcast-lab-no-nulls-in-batted-balls-launch-parameters).

### [effective\_speed](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#effective_speed)

### [release\_spin](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#release_spin)

Spin rate of pitch tracked by Statcast.

### [release\_extension](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#release_extension)

### [arm\_angle](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#arm_angle)

The angle between a line parallel to the ground and a l line extending from the location of the pitcher's throwing shoulder and the location of the ball at the time of the pitch.

### [attack\_angle](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#attack_angle)

Attack Angle measures the vertical angle of the direction in which the sweet spot of the bat is traveling as it impacts the ball, as compared to the ground.

### [attack\_direction](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#attack_direction)

Attack Direction measures the horizontal angle of the direction in which the sweet spot of the bat is traveling as it impacts the ball, as compared to a line from home plate to straightaway center field.

### [swing\_path\_tilt](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#swing_path_tilt)

The vertical angular orientation of the "plane" of the swing, as compared to the ground, defined by the path of the bat in the 40 ms prior to contact, which approximates a slice of a flat disc in shape.

### [intercept\_ball\_minus\_batter\_pos\_x\_inches](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#intercept_ball_minus_batter_pos_x_inches)

Distance, in inches, between the intercept point of the bat/ball and the batter's center of mass, in the X (horizontal) direction.

### [intercept\_ball\_minus\_batter\_pos\_y\_inches](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs\#intercept_ball_minus_batter_pos_y_inches)

Distance, in inches, between the intercept point of the bat/ball and the batter's center of mass, in the Y (mound-to-plate) direction.
